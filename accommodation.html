Kenya offers a remarkable diversity of accommodation to suit every taste and budget.

Kenya's accommodation scene is as diverse as its landscapes. From ultra-luxurious safari lodges perched on the edge of the Great Rift Valley to intimate tented camps hidden in the heart of the bush, from five-star beach resorts along the Indian Ocean to budget-friendly guesthouses in Nairobi — there is something for every traveler.

Most safari lodges and camps operate on a full-board or all-inclusive basis, covering meals, game drives, and activities. Beach resorts often offer half-board or full-board options. In cities and towns, bed-and-breakfast is the standard rate type.

We've categorized the main types of accommodation below to help you decide what suits your travel style best.

👑 Luxury

Luxury Safari Lodges
World-Class Wilderness Living

The pinnacle of African safari accommodation.

Kenya is home to some of the most renowned luxury safari lodges in Africa. Properties like Angama Mara (perched on the Maasai Mara escarpment), Giraffe Manor (Nairobi's iconic boutique hotel where giraffes join you for breakfast), and Ol Donyo Lodge (Chyulu Hills) set the standard for safari elegance. These lodges offer private plunge pools, gourmet dining under the stars, and expert guides who tailor every game drive to your interests.

Rates typically range from $800 to $2,500 per person per night, all-inclusive. Most luxury lodges are intimate, with 8-15 rooms, ensuring personalized service and exclusivity. Many also offer unique experiences like sundowner cocktails on the plains, bush breakfasts, and guided walking safaris.

⛺️ Camping

Tented Camp Experience
Sleep Under the African Sky

The quintessential safari accommodation — canvas meets comfort.

Tented camps offer the most immersive safari experience. From classic canvas camps to "glamping" with king-size beds and en-suite bathrooms, the range is impressive. Kichwa Tembo in the Mara, Saruni Samburu, and Elsa's Kopje (Meru National Park) are standout examples. Falling asleep to the sounds of the bush and waking to birdsong is an experience unlike any other.

Tented camps are typically seasonal (open during the dry season from June to October and December to March) and are designed to have minimal environmental impact. Rates range from $300 to $1,200 per person per night, often including meals, drinks, and game drives.

🌊 Beach

Beach Resorts
Coastal Relaxation

Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline offers world-class beach accommodation.

After a safari, there's no better way to unwind than on Kenya's pristine beaches. Diani Beach, Watamu, and Malindi are home to outstanding resorts such as Alfajiri Villas, Kinondo Kwetu, and Hemingways Watamu. These properties offer white sand beaches, coral reef snorkeling, Swahili-inspired spas, and exceptional seafood dining.

Beach resort rates range from $150 per night for comfortable mid-range options to $1,000+ per night for luxury villas with private pools and butler service. Many resorts offer "safari and beach" packages that combine a few days in the bush with a coastal stay.

💰 Budget

Budget & Mid-Range
Affordable Stays

Kenya offers excellent value for budget-conscious travelers.

Traveling on a budget doesn't mean compromising on experience. Kenya has a growing selection of mid-range lodges, guesthouses, and hostels. In Nairobi, areas like Westlands and Kilimani offer boutique hotels from $60-120 per night. Near the parks, mid-range lodges such as Mara Sopa Lodge, Amboseli Sopa, and Lake Nakuru Lodge provide comfortable rooms with pool and restaurant from $120-250 per person per night.

For the ultimate budget option, camping safaris are popular with backpackers. Organized camping trips start from $120-180 per person per day including meals, park fees, and transport. Basic bandas (thatched huts) in national parks offer self-catering options from as little as $30 per night.

💡 Tips

Booking Tips
Get the Best Deals

Smart tips for booking your stay in Kenya.

Book early for peak season (July-October and December-January). The best lodges fill up months in advance, especially during the Great Migration. For green season travel (April-May), you can often find excellent discounts of 30-50% off peak rates.

Use a reputable tour operator for booking safari accommodation — they often have access to exclusive rates and can handle logistics like transfers and park fees. Direct bookings are fine for city hotels and beach resorts.

Check cancellation policies carefully. Many lodges offer free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival, but peak season bookings may have stricter terms. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
