✈️ Flights

International Flights
Your Gateway to Kenya

We handle every travel detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

The primary international gateway is Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) in Nairobi, one of East Africa's busiest aviation hubs. JKIA receives direct flights from major cities including London, New York, Dubai, Doha, Amsterdam, Paris, Istanbul, Johannesburg, Mumbai, Bangkok, and Beijing. The airport is modern, with duty-free shopping, lounges, and currency exchange facilities.

Moi International Airport (MBA) in Mombasa serves the coastal region with direct flights from European cities like London, Frankfurt, and Zurich, as well as regional connections from Dubai and other African capitals. This is a convenient option for travelers heading straight to the beach.

Major airlines serving Kenya include Kenya Airways (the national carrier), British Airways, KLM, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Ethiopian Airlines, Turkish Airlines, and RwandAir. Kenya Airways has an extensive African network and operates codeshare agreements with many global carriers.

🛩 Domestic

Domestic Flights
Connecting Kenya

We handle every travel detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

Domestic flights are the most efficient way to travel between Kenya's major destinations. Several airlines operate scheduled and charter services to over 20 airstrips across the country. Safarilink Aviation, AirKenya Express, and Fly540 are the primary domestic carriers, offering multiple daily flights to the Maasai Mara, Amboseli, Samburu, Lamu, Diani Beach, and other popular destinations.

Flight times are short � Nairobi to the Mara is approximately 45 minutes, Nairobi to Mombasa is about 90 minutes, and Nairobi to Lamu is roughly 1 hour 45 minutes. Most domestic flights operate from Wilson Airport (WIL) in Nairobi, a dedicated domestic terminal located 30 minutes from JKIA.

Baggage allowances on domestic flights are typically 15 kg per person in soft-sided bags (hard cases are often restricted on small aircraft). It's advisable to pack light and leave excess luggage at your Nairobi hotel or with a storage service.

📜 Entry

Visa & Entry
Smooth Arrival

We handle every travel detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

All international visitors must obtain an eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization) before traveling to Kenya. Apply online at the official Kenya eTA portal at least 3 business days before departure. The eTA costs $30 for standard processing and is valid for 90 days for a single entry.

On arrival at JKIA or MBA, immigration is typically efficient with dedicated counters for eTA holders. You will need to present your passport (with at least 6 months validity), a printed copy of your eTA approval, and your return or onward ticket. No visa-on-arrival is available � the eTA must be obtained in advance.

For more detailed information, visit our dedicated Visa & Entry page.

🚗 Transport

Getting Around
Exploring Kenya

We handle every travel detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

Safari Vehicles: Most safari packages include a dedicated 4x4 safari vehicle with a pop-up roof for game viewing. These Land Cruisers or Land Rovers are driven by experienced guide-drivers and provide comfortable, safe travel between parks and during game drives.

Rental Cars: Self-driving is a popular option for independent travelers. Major international rental companies (Hertz, Avis, Europcar) and local operators have offices at JKIA and in Nairobi. A 4x4 is essential for most parks and reserves. Drive on the left side of the road.

Buses & Shuttles: Modern coach services connect Nairobi to Mombasa, Kisumu, and other major towns. Companies like Mash Poa, Modern Coast, and Easy Coach offer comfortable, air-conditioned buses with wifi. Matatus (shared minibuses) serve shorter routes but are less comfortable and have safety concerns.

🚡️ Airport

Airport Transfers
From Tarmac to Safari

We handle every travel detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

Pre-booked airport transfers are recommended and can be arranged through your hotel or tour operator. Official taxis from JKIA to Nairobi city center cost approximately $30-40 (3,000-5,000 KES) and take 30-60 minutes depending on traffic. Uber and Bolt operate at both JKIA and MBA and are generally more affordable.

For passengers with domestic connections, free shuttle buses operate between JKIA and Wilson Airport, running every 30 minutes. Transit time is approximately 30 minutes. If your international flight arrives late, consider booking a Nairobi hotel overnight before your domestic connection the next morning.

Most safari lodges and camps will arrange pickup from the nearest airstrip as part of your booking. If you're on a package tour, transfers are typically included in the price.
