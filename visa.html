Kenya eTA System
What You Need to Know

We handle every detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

As of January 2024, Kenya transitioned to a fully digital eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization) system, replacing the traditional visa sticker. All travelers visiting Kenya for tourism, business, or transit must obtain an approved eTA before departure. The process is entirely online, eliminating the need to visit an embassy or consulate in most cases.

The eTA allows travelers from eligible countries to apply for authorization to enter Kenya for tourism, business meetings, or short-term visits of up to 90 days. Each application is reviewed by Kenya's Department of Immigration, and approvals are typically issued within 3 business days, though same-day processing is available for an additional fee.

Your eTA is electronically linked to your passport number, so you do not need to print or present a physical document upon arrival. However, we recommend carrying a copy of your approval confirmation as a precaution. The eTA is valid for a single entry within 90 days of issuance.

📋 Step by Step

How to Apply
Simple 5-Step Process

We handle every detail so you experience only the magic of Kenya.

1

Visit the Official eTA Portal

Go to the official Kenya eTA website (www.etakenya.go.ke). Be cautious of third-party sites that charge inflated fees.

2

Complete the Application Form

Fill in your personal details, passport information, travel itinerary, and accommodation details. Ensure all information matches your passport exactly.

3

Upload Required Documents

Upload a clear scanned copy of your passport bio-data page and a recent passport-sized photograph. Additional documents may be required for business travelers.

4

Pay the Processing Fee

Pay the applicable fee using a credit or debit card. Standard processing (3 business days) is $30. Express processing (24 hours) is $50.

5

Receive Your Approval

Once approved, you will receive your eTA confirmation via email. The authorization is electronically linked to your passport. Print a copy for your records.

Important Notes
Stay Informed

The eTA is not a visa â€” it is an electronic travel authorization. Citizens of certain countries (including Afghanistan, Somalia, and Syria) still require a traditional visa obtained from a Kenyan embassy. Check your eligibility before applying.

Children of all ages require their own eTA, even if they are included on a parent's passport. Each traveler must have an individual authorization linked to their passport number.

Overstaying your eTA validity period may result in fines of approximately $50 per month of overstay, payable at immigration upon departure. Extensions can be requested at the Department of Immigration in Nairobi.

Travelers transiting through Kenya to a third country must obtain a Transit eTA unless they remain within the airport transit area for less than 6 hours and do not clear immigration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Kenya?

Most nationalities require an approved eTA before traveling to Kenya. Citizens of visa-exempt countries (such as some neighboring African nations) may enter without one. Check the official eTA portal for your country's specific requirements.

How long does the eTA application take?

Standard processing takes 3 business days. Express processing (24 hours) is available for an additional $20 fee. We recommend applying at least 2 weeks before your travel date to allow for any unforeseen delays.

Can I extend my stay beyond 90 days?

Yes, you can apply for a visa extension at the Department of Immigration Services in Nairobi. Extensions are typically granted for up to 3 additional months. You must apply before your current eTA expires.

What if my eTA application is denied?

If your eTA is denied, you will receive a reason for the denial. You may reapply with corrected information or visit the nearest Kenyan embassy to apply for a traditional visa. Application fees are non-refundable.

Is the East Africa Tourist Visa worth it?

If you plan to visit Kenya, Rwanda, and Uganda in a single trip, the East Africa Tourist Visa at $100 is excellent value. It allows multiple entries across all three countries for 90 days, saving you from applying for separate authorizations.
